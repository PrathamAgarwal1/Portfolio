## Equations look like this

A leaky integrate-and-fire neuron's membrane potential $V$ follows

$$
\tau \frac{dV}{dt} = -(V - E_L) + R\,I(t)
$$

and it spikes when $V$ crosses the threshold $V_{th}$, then resets to $V_{reset}$.
